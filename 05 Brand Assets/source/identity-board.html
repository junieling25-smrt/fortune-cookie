Why a fortune cookie

It's the friendliest way anyone has ever been handed a message about their life. We kept that feeling and put a real Four Pillars chart inside.

The cookie is familiar all over the world, so nobody needs a glossary to walk in. The mystery comes from the night sky around it and from the chart doing real work underneath. The words stay plain, warm, and short enough to fit on a slip.

Body · Nunito 400 to 800

Rounded, friendly, and easy to read at any size.

Body copy is Nunito 400 at 16.5 to 18 px with a 1.6 line height, kept under about 65 characters a line. Emphasis comes from weight, not from color or italics.

Labels are Nunito 800, uppercase, +0.1em

Italic Fraunces is kept for one thing: the question the reader carries through the day.

House rules

Enforced by a copy check that fails the build.

Plain English only

No Chinese characters and no romanized terms on screen. Four Pillars, not BaZi jargon.

Weather, not fate

Say "may" and "today". Never "will happen", never luck, never a verdict.

Every chart is uneven

Nothing is "missing" or "broken". An element you have less of just takes a little more effort.

Short enough for a slip

Two sentences for a fortune. The deep calculation lives behind one "show me" toggle.

Human-written

Every line is written by hand. No em dashes and no "it's not X, it's Y" lines.

Cute, never childish

Rounded shapes and sparkles, yes. Faces on the cookie, zodiac clip art, red and gold luck styling, no.
