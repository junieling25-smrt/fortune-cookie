## What Fortune Cookie takes from this

1. **No account before value.** CHANI's own teardown names its account wall as the top conversion leak ([ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/chani-your-astrology-guide)). Fortune Cookie's onboarding asks six quick questions, three of them optional, then cracks your cookie. There's no sign-up at any point.
2. **An "I don't know my birth time" toggle on the form.** The teardown recommends putting this on screen, not in an FAQ ([capture](https://screensdesign.com/apps/human-design-hd-birth-chart/); [CHANI, Unknown Birth Time](https://chaninicholas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4411093003539-Unknown-Birth-Time)). It's a checkbox on the birth-time step, and the reading says the hour pillar is left out.
3. **Per-chart daily content, human-written.** Users complain The Pattern's daily notes have gone stale and AI-flavored ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thepattern.app&hl=en)). CHANI writes by hand but only to twelve rising signs ([Free & Premium Content](https://chaninicholas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500001732421-Free-Premium-Content-In-the-App)). Fortune Cookie sits between them: hand-written lines, chosen per chart per day.
4. **Show the derivation.** The teardown's counter-position to CHANI's human authority is "this line came from your chart, and here it is." In Fortune Cookie that is one plain sentence per section, rather than MING's full technical panel.
5. **Win on usefulness, not wit.** Don't fight Co-Star on clever daily one-liners. The five life areas and a few small, explained tweaks each day are the "useful" answer.

## What to avoid (unchanged from MING)

- Building a dating product or matching The Pattern's relationship feature set.
- Becoming the deep, expert-facing tool. Technical terms belong in a reference layer, never in acquisition copy.
- Weekly pricing, and ever moving free features behind a paywall. Every incumbent that did so took visible reputational damage.

## The biggest open opportunity

Every product in the set charges for adding a second person, and none rewards inviting one. The teardown flags "invite-to-unlock" relationship comparison as the highest-value experiment, which is an inference, not a proven advantage. It is the natural next feature for Fortune Cookie: two charts, questions both people answer, and no score.

## Round 2: the habit features, checked against the teardown

- **A daily ritual is the right container, and the field left it half-built.** Co-Star proved a daily push can be the product with no app open expected ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/4/20879631/co-star-astrology-app-zodiac-signs)). CHANI's journal is the only reflective surface in the set, and it fires only on New and Full Moons ([A Tour of the CHANI App](https://chaninicholas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/8711720295187-A-Tour-of-the-CHANI-App)). Fortune Cookie's daily crack, tickable tweaks, and "did you do it?" check-in ask for a small return action every day.
- **The teardown's own caution applies.** It rated daily reflection an unvalidated bet that asks more effort than a no-open push. So every habit feature here is optional: the fortune and the areas read fine if you never tick a step, and a missed day never scolds.
- **Timing needs structure, not just a daily line.** The Pattern already ships personal timing with named cycles ([Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/life/pattern-app-review-features-price)). The month calendar and the 12-month strip show a fixed, calendar-based structure, which the teardown names as the differentiated shape of this system's timing. Decade-long chapters, its strongest example, are not built yet.
- **The share image is the cheap word-of-mouth move.** The Pattern's relationship outputs are built for screenshots ([Vanity Fair](https://www.vanityfair.com/style/2021/05/the-pattern-lisa-donovan)). A designed fortune card gives people a picture to share instead of a text box, without adding a second person or a score.
